What is Cybersecurity?

INTRODUCTION :

Cybersecurity means protecting computers, networks, applications, devices, and data against unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or disruption.

Since organisations are becoming more and more reliant on technology, it has become an important aspect of modern business to protect digital systems and information.

What Does Cybersecurity Protect?

Cybersecurity helps to protect:

•	Computers and laptops

•	Servers

•	Networks

•	Applications

•	Cloud environments

•	Mobile devices

•	User accounts

•	Sensitive information

•	Business systems
